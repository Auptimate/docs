The Funds area of the Auptimate Client Portal lists every fund you have access to. You can browse the list, open a specific fund to review its details, and manage compliance tasks that apply to the fund and its participants.

## View your fund list

When you open the Funds section, you see a list of all funds tied to your account. Each entry shows summary information so you can quickly identify the fund you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Funds">
    From the main navigation, select **Funds**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    Browse the displayed funds. The list loads automatically and shows the funds available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter or search (if available)">
    Use any on-screen filters or search to narrow the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open a fund

Click any fund in the list to open its detail view. From there you can review deal information, documents, and compliance status.

<Note>
  Fund details are organized into tabs. The exact tabs depend on the fund type and your permissions.
</Note>

## Funds and compliance

Funds are connected to compliance workflows that verify participants and collect required documentation. The portal tracks compliance status for each fund so you know what is complete and what still needs attention.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Review compliance status" icon="shield-check">
    Open a fund and navigate to its compliance area to see which participants have completed verification and which documents are still pending.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Download compliance packs" icon="download">
    When available, you can export compliance documentation as a consolidated pack for record keeping or external review.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Keep participant information up to date in your profile to avoid compliance delays.
</Tip>
