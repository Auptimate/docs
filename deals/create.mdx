Creating a deal in the Auptimate Portal is a guided, multi-section form. The sections you see depend on the deal type you choose (Syndicate, Founder, or Real Estate). This page describes each section and the fields to have ready before you start.

## Before you start

Have the following ready:

- Legal details of the startup or asset entity (name, country, registration number, registered office address)
- The startup's website, industry, and a logo image
- Your fundraising round details (round type, target raise, minimum investment)
- The instrument you are using (equity, SAFE, or convertible loan note)
- Any deal documents you want to attach (term sheet, pitch deck, subscription docs)

## Form sections

Sections marked **Syndicate only** are hidden when you create a Founder deal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Startup Entity">
    Legal details of the entity you are investing in.

    - **Full legal name** and **Trading name**
    - **Type of entity** and **Country**
    - **Registration number**
    - **Registered office** address
  </Step>
  <Step title="Startup Business">
    Public-facing information about the business.

    - **Logo** upload
    - **Website** (for example, `https://auptimate.com`)
    - **Industry**
    - **Tagline**
    - Optional social URLs
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal">
    Terms of the investment itself.

    - **Subscription type** (Cash)
    - **Fundraising Round** (Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A, Series A+, Series B, Series C+, Angel, Pre-IPO, Restructuring, or Other)
    - **Deal Instrument** (Share Subscription Agreement, Share Purchase Agreement, SAFE, or Convertible Loan Note)
    - **Total Fundraising Round**, **Vehicle's Allocation**, and **Minimum Investment** amounts

    The fields below the instrument change based on your selection. Equity deals ask for share class and price per share; SAFEs ask for valuation cap and discount; convertibles ask for interest rate and maturity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Documents">
    Attach documents to the deal.

    - **Hosted Documents**: upload deal documents you want investors to sign or review
    - **Other Documents**: link to externally hosted files
  </Step>
  <Step title="Syndicate Terms">
    <Note>
      Syndicate only.
    </Note>

    - **Opportunity fee** (percentage)
    - **Carried Interest** (percentage, enter 0 if not applicable)
    - Carry partner splits
  </Step>
  <Step title="SPV Details">
    <Note>
      Syndicate only.
    </Note>

    - **Email address** for the SPV contact
    - **Entity** that will operate the SPV
    - Preferred names for the vehicle
    - Lead investor selection
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save and submit

Your deal is saved as **Draft** while you fill it in. When every required section is complete, submit the deal for review. Auptimate moves it to **Under Review**, and once approved it becomes **Open** so you can start inviting investors.

<Tip>
  You can save a draft at any time and return later. Draft deals are not visible to investors.
</Tip>
