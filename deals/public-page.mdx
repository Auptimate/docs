Every deal in the Auptimate Client Portal can have a **public page**: a shareable landing page that shows the deal's public details without requiring the visitor to sign in first. Use it to send prospective investors a preview link before you formally invite them.

## What appears on the public page

The public page shows the parts of your deal that are safe to share broadly:

- Startup business name, tagline, logo, and website
- Industry and fundraising round
- Instrument and headline terms (target raise, minimum investment)
- Any public documents you have chosen to publish (for example, a pitch deck)

Private data (individual investor details, allocations, compliance records, subscription documents) is never included.

## Enable and share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your deal">
    From the deal list, open the deal you want to publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Operate and open Vehicle Visibility">
    In the Operate tab toolbar, open **Vehicle Visibility Settings** and enable the public page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the shareable link">
    A unique slug is generated for the deal. Copy the public URL and send it to prospective investors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can turn the public page off at any time from the same visibility settings. Existing links will stop working immediately.
</Tip>

## Request-an-invite flow

When a visitor lands on your public page, they can request an invite through a contact form on the page. Requests appear in your inbox so you can review, then send a formal invite from the Operate tab if the visitor qualifies.

<Note>
  Requesting an invite from the public page does not create a participant on the deal. You still need to send them a proper invite from Operate before they can complete compliance and sign.
</Note>
