Once your deal is **Open**, you can invite investors and manage their journey from the Operate tab. This page covers sending invites, the fields you can configure per investor, and the steps each investor moves through before they are fully committed.

## Send an invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Operate tab">
    From your deal, click **Operate** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite investor">
    In the toolbar above the participant table, click **Invite investor**. A drawer titled **Invite investor** slides in from the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter contact details">
    Fill in the invitee's **Email** and **Confirm email** fields. The two must match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the investment">
    In the **Investment** section:

    - **Opportunity fee** (percentage)
    - **Total amount payable** (currency amount)
    - **Allocation** (currency amount or units, depending on your deal instrument)
    - **Portal fee** is calculated by Auptimate and shown as **Pending...** until confirmed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Review the summary and send. Auptimate emails the invitee a secure link to accept the invitation and start the compliance and signing flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can invite the same person as either an individual or through one of their registered [entities](/account/entities). The investor picks which profile to use when they accept.
</Tip>

## Investor progress steps

Every participant moves through the same five steps, tracked in the Operate participant table:

| Step | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited** | Email sent, waiting for the investor to accept. |
| **Verified** | Identity verification (KYC) completed. |
| **Confirmed** | Investment amount and allocation confirmed by the investor. |
| **Signed** | Subscription documents signed. |
| **Paid** | Funds received and reconciled. |

## Confirm an investment

When an investor accepts, they open the **Confirm Investment** form to select their investor profile (individual or entity), review the fee summary, and lock in their commitment. Once confirmed, subscription documents are generated for them to sign.

<Note>
  If an investor's status is stuck at **Verified** for a long time, they may have started but not finished the Confirm Investment step. You can nudge them from the participant row.
</Note>

## Review a participant

Click any row in the participant table to open that investor's detail view. From there you can:

- Review their investment amount, allocation, and share class
- Open their **Compliance Hub** to see CDD documents and screening results
- Resend the invite email if needed
- Adjust the allocation before the investor confirms
