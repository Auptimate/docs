In the Auptimate Client Portal, a **deal** is an investment vehicle you create to raise capital and hold assets. Each deal has a type, a status, and a group of participants (fund operators, lead investors, and investors) who move it through the lifecycle. This page explains those concepts before you create your first deal.

## Deal types

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Syndicate" icon="users">
    A group of investors pool capital into an SPV to back a single opportunity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Founder" icon="rocket">
    A vehicle used by a founder or startup for a fundraising round.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Real Estate" icon="building">
    An SPV set up to hold and syndicate a property investment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The type you pick controls which sections appear when you create the deal. For example, Founder deals skip the Syndicate Terms section.

## Deal lifecycle

Every vehicle moves through a defined set of statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You are still filling in details. Not yet visible to investors. |
| **Under Review** | Submitted to Auptimate for review before it goes live. |
| **Open** | Investors can be invited and can confirm investments. |
| **Closing** | No new investors, existing investments are being finalized. |
| **Closed** | The vehicle is fully funded and operating. |

<Tip>
  You can track where each participant sits in their own journey (Invited, Verified, Confirmed, Signed, Paid) from the Operate tab.
</Tip>

## Typical workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the deal">
    Fill in the sections for your deal type (startup, terms, documents). See [Create a New Deal](/deals/create).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite investors">
    Send email invites, set allocations, and add fees per investor. See [Invite Investors](/deals/investors).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run compliance">
    Review each investor's KYC and identity verification in the Compliance Hub. See [Compliance Checks](/deals/compliance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and confirm">
    Investors sign subscription documents inline. See [Sign Documents](/documents/sign).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operate">
    Track allocations, amounts paid, and share classes from the Operate tab. See [Manage a Deal](/deals/manage).
  </Step>
</Steps>
