Every investor on an Auptimate deal completes Customer Due Diligence (CDD) before their investment is confirmed. The **Compliance Hub** is where fund operators review each investor's CDD records: personal information, verification documents, screening results, and downloadable reports.

## When compliance runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Investor is invited">
    You send an invite from the Operate tab. The investor's status is **Invited**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investor completes verification">
    The investor completes identity verification, provides supporting documents, and answers CDD questions. Their status moves to **Verified**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review in Compliance Hub">
    You open Compliance Hub for that investor, review the records, and download reports if required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investment is confirmed">
    Once compliance is satisfactory and the investor confirms their commitment, they move to **Confirmed** and can start signing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open the Compliance Hub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a deal and go to Operate">
    Select your deal, then click **Operate** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an investor's compliance record">
    Click **Compliance** on any participant row. The Compliance Hub opens in place of the Operate view, and the sidebar stays highlighted on Operate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a profile">
    If the investor invests through multiple profiles (for example, both individually and through an entity), use the profile selector at the top to switch between them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To return to the participant list, clear the `cdd` query parameter or click **Back** at the top of the Compliance Hub.
</Tip>

## What's inside the Compliance Hub

The hub has two tabs:

### Information

The **Information** tab shows the investor's or entity's CDD profile: personal details, tax residency, source of funds, PEP and sanctions status, and any structured KYC data collected during onboarding.

### Documents

The **Documents** tab lists every supporting file the investor uploaded (proof of identity, proof of address, entity constitutional documents, and so on). Click any document to view or download it.

## Downloadable reports

From the top of the Compliance Hub you can download the standard compliance reports for the selected profile:

- **CDD Pack**: consolidated PDF of all CDD information and documents
- **eIDV Report**: electronic identity verification report from the ID check provider
- **Screening Report**: PEP, sanctions, and adverse media screening results

<Warning>
  Compliance actions are restricted to Fund Operators for the deal. If you get a permission error, confirm your role on the deal.
</Warning>

## If something goes wrong

If a download fails or the hub does not load, a **"An error has occurred. Contact customer support."** notification appears. Reload the page and try again, and if the issue persists, [open a support ticket](/support/help-center).
