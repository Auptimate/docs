This quickstart takes you from signing in to inviting investors on your first deal in the Auptimate Client Portal. Each step links to a deeper guide if you want more detail.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with a one-time code">
    Go to [app.auptimate.com](https://app.auptimate.com) and enter your email. Auptimate sends a one-time code to your inbox. Enter the code to start your session.

    See [Sign In with OTP](/getting-started/sign-in) for details on session length and account recovery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Open your profile from the account menu and add your personal details, contact info, and any investment entities you plan to invest through.

    See [Manage Your Profile](/account/profile) and [Manage Investment Entities](/account/entities).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a vehicle">
    From the Deals area, click **Create** and fill in each section of the vehicle form: basic details, structure, terms, and logo. Save the vehicle when the required sections are complete.

    See [Create a New Deal](/deals/create).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite investors">
    Open your new deal, go to the investors area, and send invites by email. Invitees can accept as an individual or as one of their registered entities.

    See [Invite Investors to a Deal](/deals/investors).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign documents">
    Once investors confirm their commitment, subscription documents appear in the Documents tab. Click **Click to Sign** on each document to complete signing inline.

    See [Sign Documents Online](/documents/sign).
  </Step>
</Steps>
