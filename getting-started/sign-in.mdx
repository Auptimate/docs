The Auptimate Portal supports three ways to sign in: email and password, Google, or a one-time code sent to your email. This page walks through each option and how to recover access if you get locked out.

## Where to sign in

Open the portal at [**app.auptimate.com**](https://app.auptimate.com) and you'll land on the **Welcome back!** screen. Everyone (fund operators, lead investors, and investors) uses the same sign-in flow.

<Note>
  Don't have an account yet? Click **Sign up** at the bottom of the sign-in screen, or accept a deal invite email to create your account automatically.
</Note>

## Email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your **Email** and **Password** on the sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Sign in**. You're taken to your dashboard once authenticated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the credentials are wrong, an alert appears at the top of the form. Try again or use one of the alternatives below.

## Google sign-in

Click the **Google** button under **or continue with**, then complete Google's sign-in prompts in the pop-up. When you return to the portal you'll already be signed in.

<Tip>
  Use the same Google account each time. Signing in with a different Google account creates a separate portal account with no access to your previous deals.
</Tip>

## One-time code

If you prefer not to use a password, sign in with a one-time code sent to your email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type your email address (for example, `user@example.com`) and request a code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Auptimate emails you a 6-digit code within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type the 6-digit code on the verification screen. You're signed in as soon as the code is verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can resend the code after 30 seconds if it doesn't arrive. Check your spam folder before resending.
</Note>

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Forgot password">
    Click **Forgot password** below the password field on the sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Provide the email associated with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You'll see the message **Check your email — we have sent a password recovery to your email**. Open the email and follow the reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Choose a new password and confirm. You can now sign in with the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting locked out

Too many failed sign-in attempts temporarily lock your account. Wait a few minutes and try again, or use the one-time code option to sign in without a password.

If you still can't get in, [contact support](/support/help-center).
