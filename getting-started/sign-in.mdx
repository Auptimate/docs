The Auptimate Portal doesn't use passwords. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your Google account. This page walks through both options and what to do if you get locked out.

## Where to sign in

Open the portal at [**portal.auptimate.com**](https://portal.auptimate.com) and you'll land on the **Welcome back!** screen. Everyone (fund operators, lead investors, and investors) uses the same sign-in flow.

<Note>
  Don't have an account yet? Click **Sign up** at the bottom of the sign-in screen, or accept a deal invite email to create your account automatically.
</Note>

## One-time code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type your email address (for example, `user@example.com`) and request a code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Auptimate emails you a 6-digit code within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type the 6-digit code on the verification screen. You're signed in as soon as the code is verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can resend the code after 30 seconds if it doesn't arrive. Check your spam folder before resending.
</Note>

## Google sign-in

Click the **Google** button under **or continue with**, then complete Google's sign-in prompts in the pop-up. When you return to the portal you'll already be signed in.

<Tip>
  Use the same Google account each time. Signing in with a different Google account creates a separate portal account with no access to your previous deals.
</Tip>

## Getting locked out

Too many incorrect codes temporarily lock sign-in. The sign-in screen shows **Too many failed attempts** with a countdown; wait until it reaches zero, then request a new code.

If you still can't get in, [contact support](/support/help-center).

## Re-verification prompt after sign-in

If you see **Your account needs to be re-verified.** after signing in, your periodic compliance review is due. You can still sign in and view your account, but some actions are limited until you finish. Click **Go to Profile** and follow the steps in [Re-verification](/account/profile#re-verification).
