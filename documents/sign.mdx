When a document requires your signature, the Auptimate Client Portal lets you complete the entire signing workflow without leaving the application. The portal embeds the Auptimate signing widget so you can review terms, fill in signature fields, and submit in one seamless session.

<Note>
  Before you sign, you must be the assigned signatory for the task. If you are viewing the portal on behalf of another user, the signing action is disabled.
</Note>

## Start a signing session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a document requiring your signature">
    Navigate to the document from your **Dashboard** tasks, the **Documents** page, or the **Documents** tab inside a deal. Look for documents tagged with the **Sign** permission and an **Unsigned/Draft** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the terms">
    Read the electronic signature disclosure shown before signing. It confirms that your electronic signature is the legal equivalent of a handwritten signature and that you agree to Auptimate's Terms and Conditions and Terms of Service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click to Sign">
    Click the **Click to Sign** button. The portal requests a secure signing access grant and loads the embedded signing widget inline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the signature fields">
    Inside the embedded widget, follow the prompts to place your signature, initials, or any other required fields. The widget guides you through every field that needs your input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    After you fill in all required fields, submit the document. The widget confirms the submission and the portal updates the document status automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after signing

Once your signature is submitted successfully:

- The document status changes from **Unsigned/Draft** to **Signed/Final**.
- The signed copy is stored against the deal and becomes available to other authorized participants.
- You are returned to your Dashboard or the previous page with a confirmation message.
- If the document requires signatures from other parties, it remains in progress until everyone has signed.

## Session renewal

If your signing session expires before you finish, the widget attempts to refresh your access grant automatically. If automatic renewal fails, click **Click to Sign** again to start a fresh session. Your previous progress inside the widget is preserved when the session renews in place.

## Third-party signing

Some documents may be configured for a third-party electronic signature platform instead of the embedded widget. In those cases:

- The **Click to Sign** button opens the external platform in a new tab.
- After you sign externally, return to the portal and click **Click here if you have signed** to confirm.
- Auptimate reviews the submission manually, so there may be a short delay before the status updates.

<Tip>
  For the fastest experience and automatic status updates, Auptimate recommends using AuptiSign for all documents when it is available.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The signing button is disabled">
  Make sure you are the assigned signatory. If you are impersonating another user or viewing on their behalf, signing is blocked for security reasons.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Could not start signing session">
  This usually means the signing access grant could not be issued. Try refreshing the page and clicking **Click to Sign** again. If the problem persists, contact Auptimate support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Signature submitted but status is still unsigned">
  For third-party signing platforms, status updates are manual and may take a short time. If you used the embedded widget and the status has not changed after a few minutes, refresh the page or check your Dashboard tasks.
</Accordion>
