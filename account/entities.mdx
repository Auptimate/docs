Many investors on Auptimate don't invest as individuals. Instead, they invest through a company, LLC, partnership, trust, or foundation. The **Entity** section of your profile is where you add and manage those entities so you can select them when you confirm an investment on a deal.

## The entity list

From **Profile → Entity**, you see every entity linked to your account with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You started adding the entity but haven't submitted. Only visible to you. |
| **Under Review** | Submitted to Auptimate; compliance team is reviewing. |
| **Accepted** | Verified and available to invest through. |
| **Rejected** | Did not pass verification. Contact support to resolve. |

Drafts appear at the top of the list. Click any row to open the entity for review; click a Draft to resume where you left off.

## Add a new entity

Creating an entity is a seven-section form. Complete them in order (you can save progress after each section).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Entity">
    Basic corporate information.

    - **Type of entity** (Company, LLC, Corporation, Partnership, LP, LLP, Trust, Foundation, Sole Proprietorship, or Other)
    - **Entity sub-type** (for example, Private Unlisted, Public Listed, Personal Investment Company, Regulated Bank, Regulated Fund Manager, Investment Vehicle, Nonprofit Organisation)
    - **Full legal name**, **trading name**, and **former names**
    - **Country of incorporation** and **registration number**
    - **Date of incorporation**
    - **Industry** (from the standard industry list, for example Finance and insurance, Real estate, Manufacturing, Construction)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact">
    The entity's contact details.

    - **Registered office** address
    - **Mailing address** if different
    - Contact email and phone
  </Step>
  <Step title="Participants">
    Confirm the owners and controllers of the entity.

    - Add each participant by **email address**
    - Set their **capacity** (director, shareholder, beneficial owner, authorised signatory, and so on)
    - Auptimate will invite anyone who does not yet have a Portal account
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tax">
    Tax residence information.

    - **Country of tax residence** (one or more)
    - **Tax Identification Number (TIN)** for each country
    - If no TIN is available, provide a **reason** (from the FATCA/CRS standard reasons)
    - **US tax classification** where relevant (Active NFFE, Active Business, Other Active NFFE, and so on)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investment">
    Expected portal activities for this entity.

    - Expected number of investments
    - Expected investment amount
    - Source of funds
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bank">
    Payment method and bank details for capital calls and distributions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Documents">
    Upload supporting documents.

    - Certificate of incorporation
    - Constitutional documents (articles, bylaws, trust deed)
    - Register of members and directors
    - Proof of registered office
    - Any additional CDD documents specific to the entity type
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can save at any point and come back later. Drafts stay in your entity list until you submit or delete them.
</Tip>

## Resume a draft

From the entity list, click the **Resume** icon on any Draft row. You return to the section you were last on with all previously entered data preserved.

## Discard a draft

Click the **Trash** icon on a Draft row and confirm. Once discarded, the draft cannot be recovered.

<Warning>
  Discard only works on Drafts. Entities that are Under Review, Accepted, or Rejected cannot be deleted from the Portal; contact support if you need to remove one.
</Warning>

## View a verified entity

Once accepted, an entity's detail page shows:

- Addresses card
- Participants section with roles and capacities
- Tax residence table
- Uploaded documents

You can now select this entity when you accept an invite to a deal and confirm an investment.
