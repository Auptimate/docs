Your **Profile** is where you keep your personal information up to date, complete identity verification, and control notifications for your Auptimate Portal account. The Profile sidebar has five sections: **Personal**, **Verification**, **Entity**, and **Notification**.

## The profile sidebar

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personal" icon="user">
    Your name, date of birth, contact details, and preferences.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Verification" icon="badge-check">
    Identity verification and CDD information. Required before you can invest.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Entity" icon="building" href="/account/entities">
    Add companies, LLCs, trusts, or foundations you invest through.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Notification" icon="bell">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  A green check next to **Verification** means your identity is verified. A red exclamation mark means one or more steps are still outstanding. Verified accounts must be re-verified periodically; see [Re-verification](#re-verification).
</Note>

## Personal

The Personal view surfaces the details you provided at signup and lets you edit them. It is the day-to-day view of your account.

## Verification

The Verification section walks you through Customer Due Diligence (CDD). You will see a four-step progress bar at the top:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login">
    You have an authenticated account (already complete once you signed in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identity">
    Complete identity verification through the embedded Persona flow (photo ID, selfie, and liveness checks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Information">
    Fill in the personal information form. See the sub-sections below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verified">
    Your submission is reviewed and marked verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once verification is complete, the Verification menu is disabled in the sidebar and shows a check mark. It becomes available again when your account is due for [re-verification](#re-verification).

### Verification sub-sections

The sidebar expands the Verification menu into six sub-sections. Each maps to a section of the CDD form:

| Sub-section | What you provide |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity document** | Passport, national ID, or driving license (uploaded via Persona). |
| **Personal details** | Title, first, middle, and last name, aliases, preferred name, gender, date of birth, country and town of birth, nationality, employment status, salary range, occupation, industry, former occupation. |
| **Contact details** | Residential and (if different) mailing address, town or city, postal code, country. |
| **Tax details** | Tax residence country, tax identification number (TIN), or reason for no TIN. |
| **Account details** | Accredited investor status, source of wealth, expected activities, expected number of investments, expected investment amount. |
| **Payment details** | Payment method and bank account details used for capital calls and distributions. |

<Warning>
  You must complete every sub-section before Auptimate can verify your account. Deals and investments are locked until verification is complete.
</Warning>

## Re-verification

Verification does not last forever. Applicable law requires Auptimate to review your identity and CDD information periodically, so your account is flagged for re-verification when its compliance review date passes. How often this happens depends on the risk level assigned to your account:

| Risk level | Re-verification due |
| --- | --- |
| **Low** | Every 3 years |
| **Medium** | Every 2 years |
| **High** | Every year |

### How you know re-verification is due

When your review date passes, your account is flagged overnight and you will see:

- A **Your account needs to be re-verified.** prompt after you sign in, with a **Go to Profile** button
- **Re-verification required** under your email in the profile menu
- A **Re-verification required** message (**Re-verify your account to continue.**) if you try an action that is unavailable until you re-verify

### What is limited until you re-verify

You can still sign in, view your deals, documents, and tasks, and contact support. Until you are re-verified, you cannot:

- Create deals or add new investment entities (these pages redirect you to Verification)
- Complete **Sign** or **Pay** tasks
- Invest in a deal or confirm an investment
- Send deal invites or change deal settings

Other tasks, such as reading documents, answering forms, and uploading requested files, remain available.

### Complete re-verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Verification">
    Click **Go to Profile** on the prompt, or open **Profile** and select **Verification**. The progress bar restarts at the **Identity** step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity again">
    Complete the Persona flow with a current ID document and selfie. While the check finishes, you will see **Finalising your identity check. The form will unlock in a moment.** If it takes longer, click **Check again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit your information">
    The information form unlocks once your identity check is processed. Your previous addresses and tax details are carried over where you have not entered new ones. Check every sub-section, update anything that has changed, and submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for review">
    Auptimate screens and reviews your submission. Once you are verified again, full access is restored and your next review date is set based on your risk level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Re-verify as soon as you see the prompt so pending signatures, payments, and investments are not delayed.
</Tip>

## Entity

The Entity section is where you add companies, LLCs, partnerships, trusts, or foundations you invest through. See [Investment Entities](/account/entities) for the full workflow.

## Notification

The Notification section has two settings:

- **Marketing consent**: toggle whether Auptimate can contact you about product updates and new opportunities.
- **Task reminder frequency**: choose how often we email you a summary of outstanding tasks. Options are **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Quarterly**.

<Tip>
  If task reminder emails feel too frequent or too rare, adjust the frequency here rather than filtering emails to spam.
</Tip>
