The Auptimate Client Portal includes a built-in Support Center where you can raise tickets, browse how-to guides, and track the status of your requests. All submitted tickets are visible from the same Help area so you can follow progress without leaving the portal.

## How to get help

You can reach support in several ways from the Help page. Choose the option that fits your need.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Submit a Request" icon="send" href="#submit-a-ticket">
    Ask a question or send feedback directly to the support team.
  </Card>

  <Card title="How-To Guides" icon="book">
    Browse guided walkthroughs for common tasks in the portal.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Check the Wiki" icon="info">
    Open the knowledge base for detailed articles and references.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Book a Call" icon="calendar">
    Schedule a call with the support team for hands-on assistance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Submit a ticket

Use the steps below to create a new support ticket from inside the portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Help page">
    From the main navigation, select **Help** to open the Support Center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit a Request">
    Select the **Submit a Request** card to open the ticket form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the request type">
    Toggle between **Ask a Question** and **Send Feedback** depending on what you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a subject and a description. The description is limited to 2,000 characters. For questions, you can also set a priority level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the ticket">
    Click **Submit** to send your request. You will see a confirmation once it is received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track your tickets

After you submit a ticket, it appears in the **Your Recent Submissions** section on the Help page.

- Click a ticket to expand it and view the full description.
- Each ticket shows its current status, priority, and ticket ID.
- Status values include **Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Waiting For User**, and **Completed**.

<Tip>
  If a ticket status changes to **Waiting For User**, open it and review any requested information so the team can continue.
</Tip>

## Response times

When you submit a question, you can set a priority. Typical response times are:

- **High**: within one business day
- **Medium**: within 1 to 2 business days
- **Low**: within 3 to 4 business days

<Note>
  Response times are estimates and may vary based on request volume and complexity.
</Note>
